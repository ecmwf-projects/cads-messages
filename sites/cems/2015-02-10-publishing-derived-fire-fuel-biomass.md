🚀 ***New Dataset Published!***  

Explore the newly released ***Fire Fuel Characteristics dataset*** [here](https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/derived-fire-fuel-biomass?tab=overview). 
This dataset can be used to link vegetation changes to [fire danger](https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.0e89c522). 
It also helps in understanding the drivers of fire activity, shifts in vegetation characteristics, and supports risk assessment.
