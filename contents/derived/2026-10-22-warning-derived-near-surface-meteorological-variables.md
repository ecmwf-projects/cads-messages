Version 3.0 of WFDE5/W5E5 will be deprecated on 1 December 2026 due to an issue affecting the "Rainfall flux" and "Snowfall flux" data. We recommend switching to the corrected data by setting "version": "3_1". From 1 December 2026, to continue using version 3.0, set "version": "deprecated_3_0". For more information, see the CDS dataset page: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/derived-near-surface-meteorological-variables?tab=overview
