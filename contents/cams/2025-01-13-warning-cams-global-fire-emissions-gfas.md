Starting from 4 December, this dataset will no longer be updated. Users who wish to continue receiving the latest data after that date should apply for an account on the ECMWF Data Portal. 
More detailed information available [here](https://forum.ecmwf.int/t/changes-to-cams-global-biomass-burning-emissions-based-on-fire-radiative-power-gfas-data-availability-on-ads-and-ecmwf-data-portal/14325).

Known issue with anomalous FRP (and emissions flux) values associated with false Aqua MODIS detection at high latitudes over the eastern Arctic for the period 9-14 August 2024. 
Watch the [Forum announcement](https://forum.ecmwf.int/t/issue-with-gfas-and-cams-global-forecast-outputs-due-to-contaminated-observations/3995) for updates.
