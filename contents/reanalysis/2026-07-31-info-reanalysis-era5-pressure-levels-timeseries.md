The dataset presented here is a subset of the full [CDS ERA5 hourly data on pressure levels from 1940 to present](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-pressure-levels?tab=overview): a selection of parameters on 13 of its 37 pressure levels (1000, 925, 850, 700, 600, 500, 400, 300, 250, 200, 150, 100 and 50 hPa). **Requirements for additional parameters or pressure levels may be considered**. Please raise your request with ECMWF Support [here](https://jira.ecmwf.int/plugins/servlet/desk/portal/1/create/202).
