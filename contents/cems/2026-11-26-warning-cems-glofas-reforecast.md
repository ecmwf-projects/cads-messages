On 10 December 2026, with the release of GloFAS v5, this dataset will be updated and the download form will change, including new options (e.g. "Timespan"), renamed variables (e.g. "River discharge in the last 24 hours" becomes "Average river discharge in the last 24 hours"), GloFAS v5 availability alongside v4, and two date options: the reference date and the hindcast date (up to 20 years in the past).

**API requests written for the current form may stop working after this date.** Please check your requests against the updated form after 10 December. More information: [GloFAS v5 release](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/CEMS/pages/554157279/Pre-operational+GloFAS+release+v5).
