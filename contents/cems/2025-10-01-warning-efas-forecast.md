Due to an error in the initial conditions, EFAS forecast data over Poland from 1 July to 30 September 2025 should not be used. Historical simulation data for this period have been re-run and are now available for re-download. Please refer to the [known issues](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/display/CEMS/EFAS+-+Known+Issues) for more information.
