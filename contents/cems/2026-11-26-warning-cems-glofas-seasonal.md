On 10 December 2026, with the release of GloFAS v5, this dataset will be updated and the download form will change, including new options (e.g. "Timespan"), renamed variables (e.g. "River discharge in the last 24 hours" becomes "Average river discharge in the last 24 hours"), and the availability of both system versions (GloFAS v4 and v5). The seasonal reforecast will be merged into this dataset, which will then cover the full period from 1981 up to real time for both versions.

**API requests written for the current form may stop working after this date.** Please check your requests against the updated form after 10 December. More information: [GloFAS v5 release](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/CEMS/pages/554157279/Pre-operational+GloFAS+release+v5).
