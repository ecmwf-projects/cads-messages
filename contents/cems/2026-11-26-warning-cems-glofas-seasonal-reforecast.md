On 10 December 2026, with the release of GloFAS v5, this dataset will be retired and its data will be available from [GloFAS seasonal forecasting](https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/cems-glofas-seasonal), which will cover the full period from 1981 up to real time for both GloFAS v4 and v5.

**API requests to this dataset will stop working after this date.** Please rewrite your requests for the GloFAS seasonal forecasting dataset. More information: [GloFAS v5 release](https://confluence.ecmwf.int/spaces/CEMS/pages/554157279/Pre-operational+GloFAS+release+v5).
