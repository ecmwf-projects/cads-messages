This dataset will soon be updated with version 6.0 of the multi-track lakes (LWL) and version 1.1 of the new single-track lakes (LWL-S). **API requests written for the current download form will stop working after the update**: the "Region" option is removed, the new options "Lake category" and "Version" are added, and every lake is requested by its name and HYLA ID (e.g. "albert" becomes "albert_hyla0000159"). The full list of lakes with their new request values is available [here](https://dast.copernicus-climate.eu/documents/satellite-lake-water-level/lake-list.csv). Version 5.0 remains available.
