The GPCP v3.3 products are currently paused at September 2024 because the IR input dataset from NOAA NCEI requires a new calibration scheme to extend past that point. Once NOAA NCEI resumes production, we expect to return to updates occuring twice a year, adding six-month periods with a six-month lag updates.
