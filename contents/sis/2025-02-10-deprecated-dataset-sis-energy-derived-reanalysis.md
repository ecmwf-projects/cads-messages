This dataset is superseded by the [Global climate and energy indicators from 1950 to present derived from reanalysis](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/sis-energy-global-reanalysis?tab=overview).
From 01 July 2026, this dataset will no longer be updated, it will not be supported by the data providers and data and documentation will be provided as is. This dataset will subsequently be deprecated in December 2026.
Users are encouraged to use our [Forum](https://forum.ecmwf.int) to raise any item of discussion with respect to this dataset.
