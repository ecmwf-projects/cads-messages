As announced on our [Forum post](https://forum.ecmwf.int/t/agera5-version-1-1-to-be-deprecated-please-use-agera5-version-2-if-not-already/14940), the superseded AgERA5 version 1.1. will no longer be updated from 17th June 2026 and will subsequently be deprecated. Should any users still be using the older version 1.1., we strongly recommend they migrate to using AgERA5 v2 as soon as possible. Meanwhile, AgERA5 v2 shall remain updated on a daily basis.
